The idea

In 1791, Jeremy Bentham published a design for a round building with a single tower at its center. From that tower, one watcher could see every cell at once. He called it the Panopticon. We built ours for the retail market. Five instruments sit in the ring, and each one watches a different front. Together they form the lifeline of competitive intelligence at Impact Analytics.
